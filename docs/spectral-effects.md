# Native spectral effects

The reusable radix-2 FFT plan in src/framework/dsp/fft.hpp precomputes bit-reversal indices and twiddles. Spectral in src/framework/dsp/spectral.hpp uses a 1024-sample periodic Hann analysis and synthesis window, hop 256, and normalization 2/3 (sum of four squared Hann windows is 3/2). Each channel has independent input/output rings and bin state, and a single FFT scratch buffer is reused serially. Storage is prepared at reset, with no per-frame allocation. The fixed 1024-sample causal scheduling delay also applies to the dry path; bypass is immediate. The OBS adapter prepares format-dependent storage on the control path and adopts latest settings at audio block boundaries without a processing mutex. Ordinary same-mode parameter edits preserve the FFT overlap-add history and 1024-sample dry alignment; explicit mode or format changes still reset state. No global timestamp/scene alignment or transition crossfade.

Gate: per-bin coherent amplitude is 4*|X|/N for interior bins and 2*|X|/N for DC/Nyquist. This calibration is exact for bin-centered sine peak amplitude, not broadband RMS or an adaptive noise estimate. A threshold selects unity gain or the attenuation floor, with frame-rate exponential opening/closing smoothing. Neighboring bins are not linked; channels are independent, which can change stereo balance in gate mode.

Freeze: capture complex spectrum on hold crossing 0.5 at the next hop, save magnitude and measured phase advance between the current and preceding live frame. Continue rotating frozen bins using the captured advance, renormalizing magnitude to avoid long-term drift. If prior phase is too weak, use nominal bin advance. DC and Nyquist are forced real. Release resumes live frames through ordinary overlap-add; not a dedicated crossfade. Freeze of complex transients may produce phase artifacts and is not a pitch shifter.

Smear: exponential temporal magnitude smoothing (10–2000 ms), retaining live bin phase when significant; quiet bins continue their last phase advance. No frequency-axis diffusion or phase locking. Effect parameters are sampled on each analysis hop; mix and output are per-sample. Four shared routes including optional Sound/Sight; source contracts unchanged.

Verification: relative RMS reconstruction error 1.00769e-7 at 48 kHz with zero attenuation; input mixture of 0.4 amplitude 1500 Hz and 0.005 amplitude 6000 Hz yields 0.4 and 5.03962e-7 after gating; a frozen 0.3 amplitude tone sustains 0.212132 RMS after silence and releases to zero. An off-bin 1172 Hz tone retains amplitude 0.3 after more than a second of silence, measured at the original frequency. Tests check dry impulse delay, channel isolation and block partition invariance at 44.1 and 96 kHz. Actual libobs stereo capture verifies all modes, freeze/release, gate attenuation, reconstruction, bypass and Sound category fixture threshold modulation/fallback. Live neural producers, installed GUI, listening quality and CPU budget not yet qualified. Fixed FFT size; adjustable resolution and phase-vocoder pitch shifting are future work.

## Pitch and frequency shifting

Two additional spectral modes use the same 1024/256 Hann WOLA analysis, output schedule and aligned dry branch. The pitch ratio is 2^(semitones/12), with a range of +/-24 semitones. Frequency shifting instead adds an offset of +/-5000 Hz. Frame duration and output sample count are unchanged. Estimate instantaneous frequency from the current and prior complex bin phase after removing the nominal hop advance. Local magnitude maxima above a relative floor of 1e-4 of the frame maximum define regions; each bin is assigned to its nearest peak. For each region, relocate the peak to the nearest destination bin and translate neighboring window lobes by the same integer offset. Accumulate synthesis phase using the shifted estimated frequency and preserve the relative phase of neighboring bins. Colliding regions sum. Shifted peaks outside (0, Nyquist) are omitted; bins mapped outside that interval are discarded. DC and Nyquist are omitted for nonzero shifts. Zero shift passes the spectrum through unchanged. The current method does not guarantee perfect amplitude or transient preservation, and it is not formant-preserving. Peak changes and shifts crossing bin boundaries may produce discontinuities or artifacts; the controls ramp over 10 ms and are sampled at hops, not a glitch-free transition guarantee.

The additional modulation destinations are appended as IDs 6 (semitones) and 7 (Hz); all previous source, mode and destination IDs remain unchanged. Scratch arrays and per-channel phase state are preallocated; no new allocation during processing. Independent channel analysis can differ for complex stereo input. No CPU-budget or listening-quality qualification.

Tests: 1172 Hz at 44.1/48/96 kHz shifted an octave up/down and +/-300 Hz; at 48 kHz measured target amplitudes 0.299999, 0.271479, 0.281558, 0.281557 for input amplitude 0.3. Unity reconstructs amplitude 0.3. A 15 kHz tone shifted up an octave and a 1172 Hz tone shifted down 1500 Hz produce zero steady-state RMS in these tests. Three-tone input 600/1200/1800 Hz at amplitude 0.12 per partial produces 1200/2400/3600 Hz amplitudes 0.118117/0.103960/0.103960 for +12 semitones, and 900/1500/2100 Hz amplitudes 0.112623/0.112623/0.103960 for +300 Hz. Anti-phase stereo proportionality and modulation block-partition invariance are checked. Real libobs capture of 1500 Hz measures target amplitude 0.4 at 3000 Hz, 0.375409 at 1800 Hz, and 0.399831 at 3000 Hz with a Sound confidence fixture driving pitch; unavailable fixture returns original amplitude 0.4. These are controlled signal tests, not proof of production voice quality or arbitrary polyphonic separation.
